How this works

  • Monthly payment is principal and interest, county property tax, insurance, HOA and PMI (conventional loans under 20% down, until you reach 20% equity). The rate is this week's average for your loan type and term unless you type your own. The typical home is the average of Zillow's 3-bed, 4-bed and single-family values (Sep 2026).
  • FHA: 3.5% minimum down. A 1.75% upfront mortgage insurance premium is added to the loan, plus a yearly premium (0.55% of the balance with under 5% down, 0.50% otherwise; 0.15–0.40% on 15-year loans). The yearly premium lasts the life of the loan unless you put 10% or more down, then it ends after 11 years. 2026 loan limits are $637,100 in Salt Lake County and $601,450 in Utah County; where the typical home is above that, the down payment is raised automatically to fit, and the list says how much more you'd need. The FHA 15-year rate is an estimate.
  • Veterans: VA loans need no down payment and no PMI. The funding fee is 2.15% for a first VA loan (3.3% if you've used one) with under 5% down, 1.5% with 5% down and 1.25% with 10% or more, and it's added to the loan. Utah's disabled-veteran exemption takes your rating × $535,459 off the home's taxable value (55% of its market value).
  • Own vs rent compares net worth after the years you'll stay. The owner pays the down payment, closing costs and all monthly costs including upkeep, and gets the home's value minus selling costs and the loan balance. The renter keeps the down payment and closing costs invested, pays rent, and invests any month their costs are lower; the owner invests any month owning is cheaper. Rent is the median of live 3–4 bed house listings.
  • Scores: Cost from your payment; Own vs rent from the net-worth gap; Safety from NeighborhoodScout's 2024 crime rates; Schools from Niche, district test proficiency and GreatSchools; drive times with no traffic from each city's center to the places you pick; Value trend from Zillow's 1-year single-family change. Each is scaled 0–100 within these 20 cities.
  • Defaults: 4% a year home value growth (Zillow Research's long-run range is 3–5%), 3% rent growth, 7% returns, 3% closing costs, 1% upkeep, 7% to sell. Everything is a snapshot from Oct 24, 2026. Neighborhoods vary a lot within a city, so use this to narrow the list, then tour streets.
