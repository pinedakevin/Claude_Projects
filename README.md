# Claude Projects

Things I've built with Claude.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|

| [Nest Egg Planner](nest-egg-planner/) ([live](https://pinedakevin.github.io/Claude_Projects/nest-egg-planner/)) | A week-by-week budget and retirement planner: emergency fund, the accounts you already have, savings by age, income in retirement, and two plans compared. Runs in your browser; your numbers stay on your device. |
